User Data Deletion & Data Safety

Publisher: Tenax Mobile Labs
Scope: All apps & games (including Arrow Escape: Tap Away)
Official Contact: tenaxlabs@atomicmail.io
Effective Date: October 18, 2026

Tenax Mobile Labs is committed to data transparency, user autonomy, and compliance with Google Play Data Safety policies. This page explains what data our games handle and provides straightforward procedures for deleting all associated records.

1. Account-Free Architecture

Games developed by Tenax Mobile Labs do not require registration, passwords, email sign-ups, or social media logins. Because we do not run user accounts on custom backend servers, we do not store customer profiles or contact databases.

2. How to Delete Your Gameplay Data (Self-Service)

All game progress, level solutions, audio settings, and high scores reside solely inside your device's isolated local storage. You can erase this data immediately at any time without waiting for developer intervention:

Method A: In-Game Settings Reset (Instant)

Open the game → tap the Gear/Settings icon → select "Reset Progress" → confirm. All unlocked levels and saved puzzle states are wiped immediately.

Method B: Android System App Storage Clear

Open your phone's Settings → Apps → select the game (e.g., Arrow Escape) → tap Storage & Cache → tap Clear Storage (or Clear Data).

Method C: Uninstalling the App

Uninstalling or removing the application from your Android device permanently and irreversibly purges the app's local sandbox, deleting all progress records.

3. How to Reset or Delete Advertising Identifiers

Our games display ads served by certified ad networks (such as Google AdMob and Appodeal). These partners use your temporary Android Advertising ID (AAID/GAID) to prevent fraud and manage ad frequency. You can reset or delete this identifier directly through your Android operating system:

  1. Open your Android device Settings.
  2. Scroll down and tap Google (or Privacy).
  3. Tap Ads.
  4. Tap "Delete advertising ID" (or "Reset advertising ID").

4. Formal Deletion Request via Developer Support

If you have questions regarding data handling or wish to submit a formal data deletion inquiry, contact our support desk directly:

  • Email: tenaxlabs@atomicmail.io
  • Subject Line: Data Deletion Request - [Game Name]
  • Turnaround Time: We review and respond to all formal requests within 30 days. Because no identifiable personal data is linked to your device on our servers, our support team will guide you through verifying that all local and ad-network cached references have been cleared.
