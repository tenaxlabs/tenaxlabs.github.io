Tenax Mobile Labs ("we", "our", or "us") provides Arrow Escape: Tap Away as a free, ad-supported mobile puzzle game. This service is provided at no cost and is intended for use as is.

1. Personal Data Collection

Arrow Escape: Tap Away does not require user accounts, email registration, login credentials, or phone numbers. We do not maintain private web servers or cloud databases to harvest personal identities.

All in-game progress (such as completed puzzle levels, sound preferences, and high scores) is stored locally on your Android device using standard device storage (SharedPreferences). This data never leaves your device unless you back up your entire phone.

2. Third-Party Advertising & Diagnostics

To support ongoing development and keep the game free, we display in-game banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads provided by third-party ad networks and mediation partners (such as Google AdMob and Appodeal). These third-party SDKs may automatically process non-sensitive device data to serve relevant ads and prevent fraud, including:

  • Google Advertising ID (GAID / AAID)
  • Device model, manufacturer, and operating system version
  • Approximate geographical location (derived from IP address)
  • Ad performance metrics (views, clicks, and diagnostic crash logs)

For detailed information on how our advertising partners handle and protect data, please consult their official privacy documentation:

  • Google Privacy & Terms
  • Google AdMob Policies
  • Appodeal Privacy Policy

3. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Global Standards)

Our games are intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that personal data from a child has been unintentionally transmitted via an advertising SDK, please reach out to us, and we will take immediate steps to address it.

4. Data Control & Deletion Policy

Because your game data is stored exclusively on your device, you maintain full control over your information:

  • In-Game Reset: Use the "Reset Game Progress" option inside the game Settings dialog to wipe all level records.
  • Device Storage Clear: Clear app cache and storage via your phone: Settings → Apps → Arrow Escape → Storage → Clear Data.
  • Ad ID Reset: You can reset or delete your advertising identifier at any time through Android Settings: Settings → Google → Ads → Reset or Delete advertising ID.
  • App Deletion: Uninstalling the application immediately removes all local game files and preferences.

5. Security

We do not sell, trade, or transfer any user information. All ad delivery requests handled by third-party SDKs are transmitted through encrypted HTTPS channels.

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally to reflect technical changes or regulatory updates. Any changes will be posted directly to this URL and take effect immediately upon publication.
